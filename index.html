A Black and White Story

Come with me as I tell you a story through a series of Photographs using an old Kodak 35mm Film Camera, a vision, and a dark room to manipulate images.

CHAPTER 01

Where It All Started

My imagination is a series of creative ideas on replay, when I was given a 35mm Kodak Film Camera, I first was taken back. Coming from a digital world with updated photgraphy, what was I supposed to do with this?.

Image Title: Caged In, or Out? | This image features my friend, the inspiration was to capture a cultural moment starting with the style of his hair, the added jewlery for creative effect, and the fenced background to add an extra element, as well as a dark contrast to the back of my image.

When I Started to Trust

Understanding light was my first obsticle, then understanding space, subjects, and envoirments. All of these played a big role in capturing a moment worth a thousand words. With all of that an even bigger portion plays a vital role. Trust. You cannot see what your doing to make corrections, you must trust the process, trust your settings, your vision, and most importanly yourself...

Image Title: White Space | One of my first portfolio compositions, this photo features my bestfriend sitting on the concrete to capture a subtle yet powerful image.

An Image Captured Through His Eyes

An image dosen't have to tell a singular story. It just has to make you feel something, it helps you identify a universal emotion. Not only captured but delivered to the audience. Thats what seperates a regular image from a great image.

Image Title: His Eyes | This was probably one of my last shots, I met this guy while out taking pictures. He came up to me, and imediantly his whole face told me a sotry I knew I had to capture. You look into his eyes and see a sadness that fills a soul with sorrow, but also bring a warmness to your heart. His eyes are our eyes.

Knowing How to Capture Love

Most images inflict an emotion like sadness or anger, because those emotions are what fuel, and drives us the strongest. But how do you capture something so simple and beautful without forcing it? How do you capture a feeling that is subjected to the eye of the holder?

Image Title: SincereFace | When capturing this image I wanted it to feel like he was staring back at you, that feeling when you lay next to someone you love, and get lost in the intense feeling while staring deeply into their eyes its like you've unlocked a layer of their soul.

BETWEEN THE IMAGES

Lies Your Own Story.

During these series of images, I have told you a story. But what story do these images tell you? What emotions are internally inflicted within you while scrolling through this story. We are all a reflection of these beautiful images, our inner worlds projecting within our own interpretations. Capturing a moment so beauitful it is not an easy thing to do, quite frankly you'll never really know if what you have captured really means anything. Or does it all mean something and everything captured is beautiful, no matter the image? Thats the beauty of photography, there is not one answer. Its up to you to decide.

SincerelySyrinee

Universal Beauty

Somethings are just so beautiful, the picture naturally forms for you. Its knowing how to take your beautiful things and make them tell a story deeper than the human eye can physically see.

Image Title: Gia Trees | This image features my little sister, one of my favortie subjects to shoot. She has one of those faces that bless the camera, this was a an easy moment to capture, the position of her body, striking pose on her face, and background element all tied together very well to create this beautiful masterpiece.

An image is much more than a visual story.

All images relay a message to you, its up to you to determine what that message might be. How deep does your mind really go?

Angles, take an image and creates a visual story.

Knowing how to capture an image from a different angle adds a differnt perspective to a regular image.

Image Title: On The Run | This image was taken from an squatting poistion with my camera angled upwards. I wanted to capture an image that distorts reality which I feel like i captured.

The Turning Point

Now that I've told you my story, continue to follow me down this page to let these images speak for themsevles, live throguh my images, allow them to reveal themsevles to you. They'll tell you their inner most secrets, and the things they fear most.

Eye of the Coin

Right, am I right? Wright, rite, or write? Cover the right, you've now lost your inner sight. What was once so bright has been converted into the blackest light. There is no more within. Only left with left. Think logically, tell me a plan. You can no longer go right, even when all is rite.

Alone or A Lone?

Noise fills every space of my life, in a dark room filled with silence everything starts to bleed out so loud. Am i always alone, even surronded by those I trust is it still only me? Alone or A Lone, does it really matter?

Familia

All we have is laughter, in this life and after. I'll sit and ponder on the memories, that filled every chapter. A built in bestfriend, born with me from the start. You've known every different version of my heart, and still you stay even when we are apart. My role model, my love, my most purest form of art.
